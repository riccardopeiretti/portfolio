# Human-Centered Design & Digital Innovation

Projects focused on designing digital solutions by combining user needs, data-driven insights, and technology. These projects explore how interactive systems and digital innovation can support decision-making, learning experiences, and organizational transformation.

---

### [Designing a Quiz-Based Educational Dashboard](Designing_a_Quiz-Based_Educational_Dashboard.pdf)

- **Objective:**  
  Design and prototype an educational dashboard with an integrated quiz system to support students' learning process, self-assessment, and motivation throughout the semester.

- **Outcome:**  
  Developed an interactive dashboard concept that transforms quiz interactions into meaningful learning analytics, including performance metrics, time spent, accuracy, and progress tracking.

  The design process started from initial sketches and user scenarios, evolving into a high-fidelity prototype developed in Figma. The dashboard enables students to monitor their overall progress, identify areas for improvement, and receive insights to better prioritize their study activities.

- **Skills:**  
  UX/UI Design | Data Visualization | Learning Analytics | Prototyping | User-Centered Design

### [Enterprise Architecture for Enterprise Innovation: The Case of Sentimate](Enterprise_Architecture_for_Enterprise_Innovation.pdf)

- **Objective:**  
  Develop an enterprise architecture model for Sentimate, an AI-driven SaaS solution designed to support workplace wellbeing and improve HR decision-making processes.

- **Outcome:**  
  Analyzed the relationships between stakeholders, organizational processes, and the proposed AI-based solution. The project involved modeling the current enterprise context, identifying dependencies, and designing a future scenario where Sentimate supports recruitment, team formation, workload distribution, and employee wellbeing.

  The final model provided a structured representation of how digital innovation can transform organizational processes and create value for different stakeholders.

- **Skills:**  
  Enterprise Architecture | Business Process Modeling | Digital Innovation | Stakeholder Analysis | AI Systems Design
